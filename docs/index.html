Featured case: FKTN SVA

Fukuyama congenital muscular dystrophy
Coding exonUTRInsertion (▶ sense, ◀ antisense)Exon affected in this case

The ancestral SVA in the fukutin 3' UTR (exon 10) provides a strong splice acceptor that pairs with a rare alternative donor in exon 10, splicing out the 3' end of the coding region and proximal 3' UTR and adding SVA sequence; the protein loses its C-terminus and gains 129 SVA-encoded amino acids and mislocalizes. AONs against the SVA acceptor/ESE/ISE restored normal splicing and function.

Alu

164

Primate SINE, about 300 bp. Non-autonomous: mobilised by L1 machinery. The most frequent class of disease insertion.

L1

66

LINE-1, up to 6 kb full length and the only autonomous human retrotransposon. Disease insertions are usually 5′-truncated.

SVA

58

SINE-VNTR-Alu composite, up to ~3 kb. Hominid-specific; several founder insertions cause recessive disease.

Other

15

HERV, processed pseudogenes (retrocopies of mRNAs), polyA-only insertions, and insertions whose element the paper did not name.
