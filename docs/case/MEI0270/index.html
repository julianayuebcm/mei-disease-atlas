What the papers report

Clinical and molecular analysis in families with autosomal recessive osteogenesis imperfecta identifies mutations in five genes and suggests genotype-phenotype correlations.
Coding disruption An AluYb8 element inserted in SERPINF1 exon 6 was the pathogenic change in one AR-OI family; disruption of the coding sequence is presumed from the exonic location (not described in abstract).
“Of note, insertion of an AluYb8 repetitive element was detected in exon 6 of SERPINF1.”
