What the papers report

11 reports
Nikopoulos et al. 2019 · Nat Commun · PMID 31253780 · coded from full text
A frequent variant in the Japanese population determines quasi-Mendelian inheritance of rare retinal ciliopathy.
Coding disruption A 328-bp Alu insertion in RP1 exon 4 disrupts the reading frame and introduces a premature stop (p.Tyr1352Alafs*9); found in 16 unrelated Japanese HRD patients, 6 homozygous and 6 compound heterozygous with c.4196del or p.Arg1933*, and in 1/524 controls.
“This insertion caused the disruption of the reading frame by introducing 328 additional nucleotides, including a premature termination codon in the canonical RP1 coding sequence.”
Numa et al. 2020 · Sci Rep · PMID 33247286 · coded from full text
EYS is a major gene involved in retinitis pigmentosa in Japan: genetic landscapes revealed by stepwise genetic screening.
Coding disruption The known RP1 exon 4 Alu insertion introduces a frameshift and premature termination codon (p.Y1352Afs*9); screened by Sanger in 220 Japanese RP patients, found in 6 (3 homozygous, 3 heterozygous), solving 5 as AR RP (two compound heterozygotes with c.4196del or c.5797C>T).
“We detected the Alu element insertion including a premature termination codon in the coding region (p.Y1352A fs*9) in six patients and genetically solved five”
Mizobuchi et al. 2021 · J Clin Med · PMID 34073704 · coded from full text
Genotype-Phenotype Correlations in RP1-Associated Retinal Dystrophies: A Multi-Center Cohort Study in JAPAN.
Coding disruption The known Alu insertion in RP1 exon 4 causes a frameshift (p.Tyr1352AlafsTer9); in this Japanese cohort it was the most frequent pathogenic RP1 allele (16/50 alleles), homozygous in 4 patients (3 families) and compound heterozygous in 8 (8 families) with AR-RP or AR-COD/CORD.
“The Alu element insertion was the most frequently observed pathogenic variant (32.0%, 16/50 alleles) in our Japanese patients with RP1-associated retinal dystrophies.”
Won et al. 2021 · Sci Rep · PMID 34183725 · coded from full text
In Silico identification of a common mobile element insertion in exon 4 of RP1.
Coding disruption A 328-bp AluY insertion between c.4052 and c.4053 in RP1 exon 4 causes a frameshift (p.Tyr1352Alafs*9); found in trans with a truncating RP1 variant in five Korean probands with macular/cone-rod dystrophy.
“it revealed AluY c.4052_4053ins328/p.(Tyr1352Alafs*9) insertion in RP1 exon 4”
Tachibana et al. 2022 · Genes (Basel) · PMID 35205402 · coded from full text
Maternal Uniparental Isodisomy of Chromosome 4 and 8 in Patients with Retinal Dystrophy: SRD5A3-Congenital Disorders of Glycosylation and RP1-Related Retinitis Pigmentosa.
Coding disruption The known Japanese 328-bp Alu insertion in RP1 exon 4 causes a frameshift (p.Tyr1352Alafs); the patient was homozygous owing to suspected maternal uniparental isodisomy of chromosome 8 (mother heterozygous). No RNA study.
“Alu insertion into exon 4 of RP1, which would be missed by WES, may be among the major causes of RP in Japan [27].”
Surl et al. 2024 · JAMA Netw Open · PMID 38819824 · coded from full text
Clinician-Driven Reanalysis of Exome Sequencing Data From Patients With Inherited Retinal Diseases.
Coding disruption The recurrent East Asian Alu insertion in RP1 (c.4052_4053ins328), missed in initial exome analysis, was found by MEI-targeted reanalysis in 5 unrelated patients, each compound heterozygous with p.Arg1933*, c.4582_4585del or c.4196del.
“A customized grep program developed by our group was also applied to detect Alu insertion in RP1, a common insertion in East Asian.”
Jang et al. 2024 · Sci Rep · PMID 39448676 · coded from full text
Identification of diagnostic challenges in RP1 Alu insertion and strategies for overcoming them.
Coding disruption The founder 328-bp AluY in the last exon (exon 4) of RP1 was found heterozygous in 6/411 Korean IRD patients, all with a second RP1 pathogenic variant in trans; it is expected to escape NMD and disrupt the last 758 amino acids. The paper highlights allele dropout causing false homozygosity of c.4196del.
“All patients with AluY were confirmed to have RP1 pathogenic variants on the paired allele.”
Kwon et al. 2024 · Genes (Basel) · PMID 39457414 · coded from full text
Detecting Alu Element Insertion Variant in RP1 Gene Using Whole Genome Sequencing in Patients with Retinitis Pigmentosa.
Coding disruption The known RP1 exon 4 Alu insertion (c.4052_4053ins328, frameshift p.Tyr1352Alafs) was detected by WGS in three Korean RP patients, each compound heterozygous with a truncating RP1 variant (c.4196delG or c.6181del); maternal inheritance shown in available families.
“We report three cases of Korean retinitis pigmentosa (RP) patients with compound heterozygous variants including Alu element insertion in the RP1 gene”
Cho et al. 2026 · Invest Ophthalmol Vis Sci · PMID 42847597 · coded from full text
Allelic Architecture and Clinical Spectrum of RP1-Associated Retinal Dystrophies in a Multicenter Korean Cohort.
Coding disruption The recurrent Alu insertion in RP1 exon 4 causes a frameshift (p.Tyr1352Alafs*9); detected by IGV soft-clip review, Alu-PCR and long-read WGS in 14% of 143 Korean RP1 patients (about 20), in trans with other truncating alleles in AR RP, macular or cone(-rod) dystrophy.
“The Alu insertion c.4052_4053ins328 (p.Tyr1352Alafs*9) was carried by 14% of patients.”
Nishiguchi et al. 2020 · Jpn J Ophthalmol · PMID 32193659 · coded from abstract
A founder Alu insertion in RP1 gene in Japanese patients with retinitis pigmentosa.
Coding disruption A founder 328-bp Alu insertion in RP1 coding sequence causes a frameshift (p.Tyr1352Alafs); 5 of 26 RP patients with a heterozygous RP1 truncating variant also carried it, re-classifying 3 as autosomal recessive.
“The founder Alu insertion in RP1 is an important cause of autosomal recessive RP in Japanese patients and can be missed in standard targeted resequencing.”
Hayashi et al. 2026 · Ophthalmic Genet · PMID 41290015 · coded from abstract
Limitations of short-read NGS in detecting RP1 Alu insertions: a case emphasizing Sanger confirmation.
Coding disruption A girl with early-onset RP carried a homozygous pathogenic RP1 Alu insertion in exon 4, missed by panel and exome pipelines and found by reduced coverage in IGV; effect inferred from location (functional consequence not studied in this report).
“IGV review revealed a markedly reduced read coverage within exon 4 of RP1 in both datasets, suggesting the homozygous Alu insertion.”
