What the papers report

1 report
Li et al. 2026 · Hum Mutat · PMID 42079344 · coded from full text
X-Linked Hypophosphatemia Caused by a New Partial Insertion of LINE-1 in the PHEX Gene.
Coding disruption A 483-bp insertion (62-bp poly-T plus a 421-bp partial, mostly reverse-oriented L1 sequence) in PHEX coding exon 22 (c.2180_2181) disrupts the coding sequence; classified pathogenic (PVS1). Inherited from the carrier mother; no RNA study.
“Analysis of the mutant sequences revealed a 483‐bp insertion at chrX:22,247,883 within exon 22, which was also present in the proband′s mother, indicating her carrier status for the mutation.”
