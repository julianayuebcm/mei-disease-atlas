What the papers report

2 reports
Zhang et al. 2000 · Hum Mutat · PMID 10737976 · coded from abstract
AluY insertion (IVS4-52ins316alu) in the glycerol kinase gene from an individual with benign glycerol kinase deficiency.
Not described An AluY inserted 52 bp upstream of exon 5 (intron 4) is reported as the cause of benign glycerol kinase deficiency; the abstract does not describe the RNA consequence.
“DNA sequencing of this subject's glycerol kinase gene showed the insertion of an AluY sequence in intron 4 of the glycerol kinase gene.”
Zhang et al. 2006 · Pediatr Res · PMID 16549535 · coded from abstract
Asymptomatic isolated human glycerol kinase deficiency associated with splice-site mutations and nonsense-mediated decay of mutant RNA.
Aberrant splicing An Alu insertion in GK intron 4 led to alternative splicing; misspliced RNA was apparently targeted by NMD, with residual normal transcript explaining the mild phenotype.
“A fourth patient had an Alu insertion in intron 4 that led to alternative splicing.”
