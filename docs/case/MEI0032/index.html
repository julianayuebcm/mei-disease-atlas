What the papers report

3 reports
Akman et al. 2010 · Neuromuscul Disord · PMID 20471263 · coded from full text
Neutral lipid storage disease with subclinical myopathy due to a retrotransposal insertion in the PNPLA2 gene.
Coding disruptionExpression loss A ~1.8-kb SVA retrotransposon interrupts exon 3 of PNPLA2 (homozygous; parents heterozygous). No PNPLA2 cDNA could be amplified from patient leukocytes or fibroblasts; authors suggest the polyA/GC-rich VNTR sequences block transcription.
“RT-PCR failed to amplify the patient’s PNPLA2 mRNA.”
Taniguchi-Ikeda et al. 2011 · Nature · PMID 21979053 · coded from full text
Pathogenic exon-trapping by SVA retrotransposon and rescue in Fukuyama muscular dystrophy.
Exonization Previously reported SVA insertion in PNPLA2; RT-PCR of patient fibroblast cDNA showed aberrant splicing into the SVA (exon-trapping).
“Moreover, we observe exon-trapping in other SVA insertions associated with disease (hypercholesterolemia4, neutral lipid storage disease5) and human-specific SVA insertion in a novel gene.”
