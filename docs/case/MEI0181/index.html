What the papers report

1 report
de Bruijn et al. 2025 · NPJ Genom Med · PMID 40263280 · coded from full text
Long-read technologies identify a hidden LINE-1/ERV1 insertion in IQCB1 as causative variant for Senior-Løken syndrome.
Exon skippingExonization A ~6.2-kb combined LINE-1/ERV1 element (copy of a chr1 segment, with polyA and short duplication) in IQCB1 intron 6, in trans with a frameshift; targeted long-read cDNA sequencing showed a 276-bp pseudoexon from the mobile element together with exon 6 skipping, introducing a premature stop codon.
“A complex splice defect was observed in the patient’s cells: part of the mobile element was recognized as a pseudoexon (276 bp) in combination with skipping of exon 6.”
