What the papers report

1 report
Castro et al. 2020 · Front Neurol · PMID 33408686 · coded from full text · low confidence
Case Report: Early-Onset Behavioral Variant Frontotemporal Dementia in Patient With Retrotransposed Full-Length Transcript of Matrin-3 Variant 5.
Not described A retrotransposed full-length cDNA of MATR3 transcript variant 5 (seen on the panel as an apparent exon 15 duplication) is inserted on chromosome 12, ~20 kb upstream of LHX5, flanked by 15-bp repeats; it is also carried by an unaffected parent and grandparent, so it may be a reduced-penetrance cause or benign. No expression or functional effect was demonstrated.
“This suggests that the mutation is either a disease-causing mutation with incomplete penetrance, which has been observed in heritable FTD, or a benign variant.”
