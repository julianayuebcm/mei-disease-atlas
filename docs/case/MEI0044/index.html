Samuelov et al. 2011 · Br J Dermatol · PMID 21332462 · coded from abstract
An exceptional mutational event leading to Chanarin-Dorfman syndrome in a large consanguineous family.
Aberrant splicingIntron retention No coding-sequence mutation was found, but RT-PCR on patient skin RNA showed a 101 bp insertion between exons 3 and 4 in all four patients of a large consanguineous family. Sequencing and bioinformatics traced this to a LINE element inserted into intron 3, which causes aberrant splicing of the mutant intron 3.
“Bioinformatic analysis and direct sequencing indicated that this insertion resulted from an exceptional mutational event, namely, the insertion of a LINE element into intron 3 of the ABHD5 gene, leading to aberrant splicing out of the mutant intron 3.”
