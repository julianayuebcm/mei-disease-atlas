What the papers report

2 reports
Pfeifer et al. 2026 · J Med Genet · PMID 42055800 · coded from full text
Pathogenic mobile element insertion in the MEN1 gene mimicking a deletion in MLPA: characterisation by long-read sequencing.
Coding disruption An AluYb8 inserted in inverted (antisense) orientation within MEN1 exon 2 at p.Leu48 (16-bp TSD, ~44-nt polyA) introduces a premature stop codon, predicted to cause NMD or a truncated protein retaining ~8% of MEN1; it disrupted the MLPA probe site, mimicking an exon 2 deletion, and cosegregated with MEN1 in two families.
“In silico analysis revealed that the MEN1:c.143_144insAlu leads to the premature appearance of a stop codon in the MEN1 gene.”
Cragg et al. 2026 · Cancer Genet · PMID 41421069 · coded from abstract
Multiple endocrine neoplasia type 1 syndrome due to novel Alu insertion.
Not described A novel Alu insertion in MEN1 identified by NGS in a family with four affected members over two generations; location and mechanism not described in the abstract.
“a family with four affected members over two generations who have been diagnosed with the syndrome after next generation sequencing identified a novel Alu insertion.”
