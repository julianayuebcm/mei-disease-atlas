What the papers report

2 reports
den Hollander et al. 1999 · Nat Genet · PMID 10508521 · coded from abstract
Mutations in a human homologue of Drosophila crumbs cause retinitis pigmentosa (RP12).
Coding disruption Among ten unrelated RP12 patients screened, one carried a homozygous AluY insertion that disrupts the CRB1 open reading frame. The abstract does not localise the insertion to a numbered exon and no transcript study is described.
“In ten unrelated RP patients with preserved para-arteriolar RPE, we identified a homozygous AluY insertion disrupting the ORF, five homozygous missense mutations and four compound heterozygous mutations in CRB1.”
