Multi-omics analysis in suspected hereditary breast and ovarian cancer cases reveals novel candidate susceptibility factors.
Coding disruption Rare L1 insertion in POLK exon 15 (last exon) in a patient with ovarian and Luminal-A breast cancer; presumed to disrupt the gene but WTS/RT-PCR showed no splicing change and no functional effect was demonstrated.
“The L1 element insertion in exon 15 of the POLK gene seq[GRCh38] der(5)ins(5;?)(q13.3;?) NC_000005.10:g.75,598,292_75,598,293insL1 was identified in patient P23, who has been diagnosed with OC and Luminal-A BC.”
