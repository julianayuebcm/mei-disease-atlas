What the papers report

1 report
Weiß et al. 2025 · Hum Gene Ther · PMID 40257001 · coded from full text
Deep Intronic SVA_E Retrotransposition as a Novel Factor in Canavan Disease Pathogenesis.
Aberrant splicingExpression loss A ~2,630-bp deep intronic SVA_E in ASPA intron 4, in trans with coding variants and on a shared haplotype with intron 1 variant c.236+1187A>C, leads to loss of transcripts from the SVA allele (coding variants appear homozygous in mRNA), consistent with efficient mRNA degradation; the authors suggest the SVA creates a new splice acceptor.
“Functional characterization of this SV at the mRNA level revealed homozygosity for the specific coding variants of each individual patient, which must be accompanied by a loss of the transcripts altered by the SVA_E retrotransposition.”
