What the papers report

1 report
Bruellman et al. 2020 · Thyroid · PMID 31868128 · coded from abstract
Insertion of an Alu Element in Thyroglobulin Gene as a Novel Cause of Congenital Hypothyroidism.
Coding disruption Two of four siblings in a consanguineous Sudanese family with congenital hypothyroidism, goiter, high thyrotropin and undetectable serum thyroglobulin carried a novel Alu element inserted within an exon of TG, producing a frameshift (p.Y3637Ffs). The exon number and transcript consequences are not given.
“a novel frameshift insertion of an Alu element within an exon of the TG gene: c.7909ins p.Y3637Ffs”
