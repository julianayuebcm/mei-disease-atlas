What the papers report

1 report
Song et al. 2024 · Mol Genet Genomic Med · PMID 38433605 · coded from full text
A case report of an Egyptian family with familial hypercholesterolemia and an exonic LINE-1 insertion in LDLR.
Coding disruption A predicted 918-bp 5'-truncated, reverse-oriented L1 (L1 bases 5141-6059 plus poly(A)) with an 8-bp TSD inserted in LDLR exon 7; homozygous in a severely affected proband and heterozygous in mildly affected parents; expected to disrupt the coding sequence but not validated at RNA or protein level.
“Although it could not be validated at the transcript or protein level, this insertion is expected to disrupt the function of the gene (Hancks & Kazazian, 2012) which is consistent with the known disease mechanism.”
