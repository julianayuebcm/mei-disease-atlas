Expanding the genetic landscape of inherited metabolic diseases using long-read sequencing and transcriptomic profiling.
Expression lossOther aberrant transcript A 2.5-kb L1 inserted 7.6 kb downstream of SLC2A1 rewires 3D chromatin: 4C-seq in patient fibroblasts shows strongly reduced contacts between the SLC2A1 promoter and its 5' distal cis-regulatory element cluster (new loop to the TAD 3' border), with SLC2A1 expression ~20% of normal.
