What the papers report

1 report
Helm et al. 2023 · Eur J Med Genet · PMID 37264881 · coded from full text
Disruption of FBN1 by an Alu element insertion: A novel genetic cause of Marfan syndrome.
Coding disruption A heterozygous ~337-bp AluYa5 inserted in FBN1 coding exon 54 is predicted to cause a frameshift (p.Glu2189delinsPhe...Ter) without copy-number change; segregates with Marfan syndrome in father and two sons. RNA/protein consequences were not tested.
“Sanger sequencing results verified the Alu insertion in the FBN1 exon 54 of subject IU-TAA-882.”
