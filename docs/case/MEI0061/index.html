What the papers report

1 report
Pozojevic et al. 2024 · Sci Rep · PMID 39009627 · coded from full text
LINE1-mediated epigenetic repression of androgen receptor transcription causes androgen insensitivity syndrome.
Expression lossEpigenetic silencingUTR effect A maternally inherited (apparently de novo in the mother) hemizygous 2,752-bp 5'-truncated L1 in the AR 5' UTR is associated with increased CpG methylation around the insertion and markedly reduced AR mRNA and protein in patient genital skin fibroblasts; plasmid assays showed the L1 reduces AR expression more than a size-matched control insert.
“We show that this insertion causes a strongly reduced AR mRNA expression both through the disruption of regulatory sequences in the 5′UTR and through DNA methylation at the insertion site,”
