What the papers report

1 report
Ferraro et al. 2026 · HGG Adv · PMID 41017149 · coded from full text
Long-read DNA and RNA sequencing reveal an intronic retrotransposon insertion in TCOF1 causing Treacher Collins syndrome.
ExonizationOther aberrant transcript A 3,396-bp hybrid SVA_C/SVA_E deep in TCOF1 intron 17 (just downstream of the minor isoform c) is partially exonized (~750 bp of SVA included with a frameshift/stop, NMD) and shifts the allele toward the short non-canonical isoform c, reducing canonical TCOF1. Two paternal half-siblings inherited it from their unaffected gonadosomatic mosaic father.
“In both cycloheximide-treated and untreated cells, we detected the expression and incorporation of ∼750 bp of the SVA C together with TCOF1 exons (Figure 2B), in correspondence to the sites predicted by SpliceAI (Figure 3A), confirming the SVA exonization, already described for other pathogenic SVA insertions.”
