What the papers report

Brouha et al. 2002 · Am J Hum Genet · PMID 12094329 · coded from abstract
Evidence consistent with human L1 retrotransposition in maternal meiosis I.
Not described A truncated L1 with a 3' transduction, derived from the source element LRE3 on 2q24.1, inserted into CYBB on the patient's maternal X; the insertion most likely arose in maternal meiosis I. The molecular consequence on CYBB is not described in the abstract.
“We characterized a truncated L1 retrotransposon with a 3' transduction that was inserted, in a Dutch male patient, into the X-linked gene CYBB, thereby causing chronic granulomatous disease.”
