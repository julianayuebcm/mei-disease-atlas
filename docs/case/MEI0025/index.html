Mechanism

Aberrant splicing primary

Other splice changes: cryptic splice sites, altered splice-site choice, pseudo-exons from flanking sequence.

Intron retention

An intron is kept in the mature mRNA.

Premature polyadenylation

A polyA signal or terminator inside the element cuts the transcript short.

Expression loss

Reduced or absent transcript or protein, without a more specific mechanism.

Epigenetic silencing

Methylation or chromatin changes spreading from the element silence the gene.

Lanikova et al. 2013 · Hum Mutat · PMID 23878091 · coded from full text
β-Thalassemia due to intronic LINE-1 insertion in the β-globin gene (HBB): molecular mechanisms underlying reduced transcript levels of the β-globin(L1) allele.
Aberrant splicingIntron retentionPremature polyadenylationExpression lossEpigenetic silencing A full-length L1 in HBB intron 2 reduces transcription of the allele (4.7-fold lower run-on rate) with promoter/enhancer hypermethylation and histone deacetylation-associated silencing; some primary transcripts are truncated at the L1, and minor aberrant transcripts (cryptic acceptors in IVS-II and in L1, intron+L1 retention) are NMD substrates.
“The lower steady-state amount of mRNA produced by the β-globinL1 allele also resulted from a reduced rate of transcription and decreased production of full-length β-globin primary transcripts.”
