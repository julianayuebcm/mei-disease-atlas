What the papers report

2 reports
Venet et al. 2017 · Hum Mutat · PMID 28945313 · coded from abstract
Severe infantile isolated exocrine pancreatic insufficiency caused by the complete functional loss of the SPINK1 gene.
Not described Homozygous insertion of a full-length inverted Alu into the SPINK1 3'UTR, stated to cause complete functional loss of SPINK1; the abstract does not describe how.
“an insertion of a full-length inverted Alu element into the 3'-untranslated region of the SPINK1 gene”
Masson et al. 2024 · Am J Hum Genet · PMID 39265574 · coded from abstract
Alu insertion-mediated dsRNA structure formation with pre-existing Alu elements as a disease-causing mechanism.
Inverted-Alu structureExpression lossUTR effect The homozygous 3' UTR Alu insertion abolishes SPINK1 mRNA; a 3' UTR reporter showed only ~50% reduction, whereas full-length gene assays showed the loss results from dsRNA structures formed between the inserted Alu and two oppositely oriented Alus in intron 3, with expression restored when all three Alus were placed in the same orientation.
“We provide compelling evidence to link the detrimental effect of Alu_Ins to extensive dsRNA structures formed between Alu_Ins and pre-existing intronic Alu sequences, including the restoration of SPINK1 mRNA expression by aligning all three Alu elements in the same orientation.”
