What the papers report

1 report
Yamamoto et al. 2021 · Eur J Hum Genet · PMID 33293698 · coded from abstract
SVA retrotransposon insertion in exon of MMR genes results in aberrant RNA splicing and causes Lynch syndrome.
Aberrant splicing An ~2.5-kb SVA inserted in exon 5 of MSH6 induced aberrant splicing detected by mRNA sequencing; one of the two SVAs studied is present in a Japanese reference genome (polymorphic), which one is not stated in the abstract.
“Both of these insertions induced aberrant splicing, and these variants were successfully identified by mRNA sequencing or visual observation of mapping results”
