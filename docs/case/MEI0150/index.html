What the papers report

1 report
Nakamura et al. 2015 · Int J Hematol · PMID 25739383 · coded from abstract
SVA retrotransposition in exon 6 of the coagulation factor IX gene causing severe hemophilia B.
Aberrant splicingExpression loss An antisense ~2.5-kb SVA_F inserted in F9 exon 6 disturbed normal splicing of exons 5 and 6 in an exon-trap assay; the authors suggest the abnormal mRNA would be reduced by NMD.
“We observed that regular splicing at exons 5 and 6 of F9 was disturbed by the SVA retrotransposition, suggesting that abnormal FIX mRNA may be reduced by nonsense-mediated mRNA decay.”
