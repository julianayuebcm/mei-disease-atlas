A 20.7 kb deletion within the factor VIII gene associated with LINE-1 element insertion.
Insertion-associated deletion A 20.7-kb deletion removing F8 exons 15-20 (intron 14 to intron 20) carries a 38-bp insertion homologous to the L1 3' end at the breakpoint; breakpoints are homologous to an L1 flanking region, suggesting retrotransposition of an L1 readthrough product accompanied the deletion.
“A 38-base insertion homologous to the 3' region of a LINE-1 (L1) element was detected at the breakpoint of the deletion.”
