Mechanism

Exonization primary

Sequence from the inserted element is spliced into the mRNA as a new exon or an exon extension.

Expression loss

Reduced or absent transcript or protein, without a more specific mechanism.

Aberrant splicing

Other splice changes: cryptic splice sites, altered splice-site choice, pseudo-exons from flanking sequence.

Coding disruption

The insertion lands in coding sequence and causes a frameshift, premature stop or disrupted protein.

What the papers report

3 reports
Walsh et al. 2021 · J Med Genet · PMID 33060287 · coded from full text
CRISPR-Cas9/long-read sequencing approach to identify cryptic mutations in BRCA1 and other tumour suppressor genes.
Coding disruptionExonization Two cryptic splice donors within the 5' Alu-like domain of the SVA pair with a cryptic acceptor in BRCA1 intron 13, producing 509-bp and 666-bp pseudoexons in the BRCA1 mRNA (exons 12-15 RT-PCR in puromycin-treated lymphoblasts) that introduce a premature stop at codon 1558.
“Sanger sequencing of the transcripts indicates that two cryptic splice donor sites within the 5’ Alu-like domain of the SVA element exploit a cryptic splice acceptor in BRCA1intron 13, resulting in exonification of segments of 509bp and 666bp in the BRCA1 message and a premature stop at codon 1558.”
Ohori et al. 2025 · J Hum Genet · PMID 40629180 · coded from abstract
Hidden SVA retrotransposon insertion in BRCA1 revealed by nanopore targeted sequencing causes hereditary breast and ovarian cancer.
Aberrant splicing An intronic SVA insertion in BRCA1 in a proband and her mother, found by targeted nanopore sequencing, causes splicing aberrations confirmed by cDNA qPCR and cDNA amplicon sequencing.
“We further confirmed splicing aberrations using cDNA quantitative PCR with TaqMan probes and Flongle cDNA amplicon sequencing.”
Staaf et al. 2019 · Nat Med · PMID 31570822 · coded from full text
Whole-genome sequencing of triple-negative breast cancers in a population-based clinical study.
Expression loss A germline SVA located 1.8 kb downstream of the first coding exon of BRCA1 (absent from 1000 Genomes) was found in an HRDetect-high TNBC with LOH of the wild-type allele and markedly reduced BRCA1 expression, proposed as a novel mechanism of BRCA1 abrogation.
“Moreover, wild-type allele LOH was noted and BRCA1 expression was markedly reduced.”
