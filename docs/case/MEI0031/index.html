What the papers report

2 reports
Gardner et al. 2019 · Nat Commun · PMID 31604926 · coded from full text
Contribution of retrotransposition to developmental disorders.
Coding disruption De novo Alu inserted into a coding exon of NSD1, predicted loss of function; clinician deemed it likely causative and the patient's blood methylation profile clusters with Sotos syndrome cases.
“Three out of four reported mutations (NSD1, MEF2C, and ARID2) were subsequently deemed to be likely causative of the patient’s phenotype (Supplementary Table 2) by the referring clinician.”
Torene et al. 2020 · Genet Med · PMID 31965078 · coded from full text
Mobile element insertion detection in 89,874 clinical exomes.
Coding disruption Alu inserted within the coding sequence of NSD1 (c.1926_1927insAlu), classified PATH by ACMG criteria; no 5' truncation. Functional/RNA effect not studied. Absent from mother; father not tested (presumed de novo).
“By applying MEI detection, an Alu insertion was detected in exon 10 of NSD1 that was absent from the mother and presumed de novo.”
