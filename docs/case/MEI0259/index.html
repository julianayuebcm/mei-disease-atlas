What the papers report

1 report
Sexton et al. 2026 · Cancer Res Commun · PMID 42685354 · coded from full text · low confidence
The Role of Germline Transposable Element Insertions in Pediatric Cancer Predisposition.
Expression lossUTR effect A unique germline full-length L1 in the PTEN 3' UTR (downstream of the 7th of 16 polyA sites) was associated in blood RNA-seq with reduced usage of the downstream polyA sites (loss of long 3' UTR isoforms) and lower PTEN TPM; causality for the tumour is proposed, not established. (A STIM1 intronic SVA with exonization in a T-ALL patient was not considered a driver and is not recorded.)
“Together, these observations suggest that the L1 insertion in the 3′ UTR of PTEN disrupts longer transcript isoforms, impairing its expression.”
