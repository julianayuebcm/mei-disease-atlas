What the papers report

1 report
Tabata et al. 2008 · J Hum Genet · PMID 18392553 · coded from abstract
Identification of 13 novel mutations including a retrotransposal insertion in SLC25A13 gene and frequency of 30 mutations found in patients with citrin deficiency.
Not described A 2,667-nt insertion in SLC25A13 intron 16 consisting of an antisense processed cDNA copy of C6orf68, flanked by a 17-nt duplication; the abstract does not describe its transcript effect. Seen in 22 Japanese families and in all East Asian populations tested.
“IVS16ins3kb identified in a Japanese CTLN2 family seems to be a retrotransposal insertion, as the inserted sequence (2,667-nt) showed an antisense strand of processed complementary DNA (cDNA) from a gene on chromosome 6 (C6orf68)”
