What the papers report

2 reports
Okubo et al. 2007 · Mol Genet Metab · PMID 17706445 · coded from abstract
A novel complex deletion-insertion mutation mediated by Alu repetitive elements leads to lipoprotein lipase deficiency.
Insertion-associated deletion A ~150 bp 5'-truncated AluYb9 with poly(A) tail was inserted at the junction of a 2.3 kb deletion (intron 1 to intron 2) that removes exon 2; the patient was homozygous, with no LPL activity or mass.
“These findings indicated that Alu retrotransposition caused the complex deletion-insertion.”
