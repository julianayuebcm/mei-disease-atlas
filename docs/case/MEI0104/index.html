What the papers report

1 report
Kherraf et al. 2018 · Am J Hum Genet · PMID 30122540 · coded from abstract
A Homozygous Ancestral SVA-Insertion-Mediated Deletion in WDR66 Induces Multiple Morphological Abnormalities of the Sperm Flagellum and Male Infertility.
Insertion-associated deletion Seven MMAF men carry a homozygous deletion removing the two penultimate exons of WDR66, with a single chimeric SVA at the breakpoint in all, suggesting an ancestral SVA insertion followed by recombination generated the deletion.
“suggesting that the initial deletion event was potentially mediated by an SVA insertion-recombination mechanism”
