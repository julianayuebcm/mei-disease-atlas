What the papers report

1 report
Borges-Monroy et al. 2021 · Mob DNA · PMID 34838103 · coded from full text
Whole-genome analysis reveals the contribution of non-coding de novo transposon insertions to autism spectrum disorder.
Coding disruption A de novo Alu insertion (15-bp TSD, polyA tail) in an exon of the LoF-intolerant ASD gene CSDE1 was found in a Simons Simplex Collection proband and considered likely pathogenic as an exonic disruption; a single clipped read in the mother raised possible low-level maternal mosaicism.
“We also detected exonic TEIs in LoF genes in cases, including a causal exonic Alu insertion in CSDE1, known ASD gene [22].”
