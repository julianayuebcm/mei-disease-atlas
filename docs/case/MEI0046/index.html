Novel Alu retrotransposon insertion leading to Alström syndrome.
Coding disruption A homozygous 333-bp AluYa5 (5'-truncated by 34 nt, 76-nt polyA, with TSD) inserted into the ALMS1 exon 16 coding sequence introduces a premature stop codon with an alternative C-terminus.
