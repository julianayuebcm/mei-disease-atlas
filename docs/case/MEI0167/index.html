Mechanism

Exonization primary

Sequence from the inserted element is spliced into the mRNA as a new exon or an exon extension.

Premature polyadenylation

A polyA signal or terminator inside the element cuts the transcript short.

Expression loss

Reduced or absent transcript or protein, without a more specific mechanism.

Transduction

Co-mobilised flanking DNA carried with the element is the functional cause.

What the papers report

1 report
Bychkov et al. 2021 · Int J Mol Sci · PMID 34639227 · coded from full text
Complex Transposon Insertion as a Novel Cause of Pompe Disease.
ExonizationPremature polyadenylationExpression lossTransduction A homozygous complex insertion (SVA_D and SVA_E in sense plus a 3'-transduced antisense L1ME3 from the chr20 CPX-1 source) deep in intron 15: GAA exon 15 splices to a cryptic AG acceptor at the end of the SVA CCCTCT repeats and the chimeric transcript terminates at a polyA site in the transduced L1ME3, sharply reducing 3' GAA mRNA (1% of control).
“Sanger sequencing of the PCR products revealed that the GAA exon 15 was spliced with TE due to activation of the acceptor site composed of the AG dinucleotide located at the end of CCCTCT repeats”
