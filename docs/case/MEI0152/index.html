What the papers report

1 report
Tighe et al. 2002 · Genes Immun · PMID 12215906 · coded from abstract
Inactivation of the Fas gene by Alu insertion: retrotransposition in an intron causing splicing variation and autoimmune lymphoproliferative syndrome.
Exon skipping A 331-bp Alu (AluSb1-like, 34-bp polyA, 17-bp TSD) in FAS intron 7 led to an mRNA lacking exon 8 without loss of a splice junction; father and son heterozygous, with defective Fas-mediated apoptosis.
“Both patients were heterozygous for the mutated allele that produced Fas mRNA lacking exon 8, although not due to loss of a splice junction.”
