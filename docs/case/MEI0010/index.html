What the papers report

3 reports
Miller et al. 2022 · J Bone Miner Res · PMID 35811283 · coded from full text
Targeted Long-Read Sequencing Identifies a Retrotransposon Insertion as a Cause of Altered GNAS Exon A/B Methylation in a Family With Autosomal Dominant Pseudohypoparathyroidism Type 1b (PHP1B).
Premature polyadenylationEpigenetic silencing A maternally inherited ~2.8-kb SVA_E inserted ~1,200 bp telomeric (downstream) of GNAS exon XL segregates with loss of methylation at the exon A/B DMR in six affected relatives. Authors propose that AATAAA signals at the SVA 3' end truncate the maternal NESP transcript, preventing re-establishment of A/B methylation in oogenesis (not directly tested).
“The retrotransposon most likely causes the observed epigenetic defect by impairing function of a maternally derived NESP transcript, consistent with findings in mice lacking full-length NESP mRNA and in PHP1B patients with deletion of exon NESP and adjacent intronic sequences.”
Iwasaki et al. 2025 · Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A · PMID 40249781 · coded from full text
Bidirectional disruption of GNAS transcripts causes broad methylation defects in pseudohypoparathyroidism type 1B.
Premature polyadenylationEpigenetic silencingOther aberrant transcript A maternally inherited ~2.8-kb SVA in intron 1 of GNAS-AS1 carries a polyadenylation signal that blunts run-through transcription from the exon H/AS region (and GNAS-AS1 in the other direction), preventing establishment of maternal GNAS imprints and causing broad loss of methylation at maternal DMRs.
“Furthermore, a retrotransposon insertion within GNAS, identified in a PHP1B kindred, impairs bidirectionally run-through transcription, thus perturbing GNAS methylation.”
Nakamura et al. 2026 · J Bone Miner Res · PMID 42925263 · coded from abstract
Targeted long-read sequencing reveals allele-specific methylation profiles at the GNAS locus in pseudohypoparathyroidism with various etiologies.
Epigenetic silencing A maternally inherited retrotransposon insertion at chr20:58,863,683 in a familial PHP1B case is associated with marked loss of methylation at the GNAS A/B-DMR and mild hypomethylation of AS1-, AS2- and XL-DMRs (an imprinting/methylation defect rather than silencing).
“The case with retrotransposon insertion (chr20:58,863,683) showed markedly hypomethylated A/B-DMR, plus mild hypomethylation of the AS1-DMR, AS2-DMR, and XL-DMR”
