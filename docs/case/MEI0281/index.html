What the papers report

1 report
Zhou et al. 2019 · Front Genet · PMID 32082363 · coded from full text
Insertion of LINE-1 Retrotransposon Inducing Exon Inversion Causes a Rotor Syndrome Phenotype.
Exon skippingInsertion-associated deletion A homozygous ~6-kb reverse-complement L1 inserted in SLCO1B3 intron 3 is accompanied by inversion of a 1,185-bp segment containing exon 4; the inverted exon loses its splice sites and is excluded from mRNA in a minigene assay, predicted to create a premature stop. The patient also carries a homozygous SLCO1B1 nonsense variant (digenic Rotor).
“Further sequence analysis revealed that a reverse-complementary LINE-1 retrotransposon (~6 kbp) was inserted upstream of the inverted DNA fragment (Figure 2B).”
