Mechanism

Exon skipping primary

The insertion makes the spliceosome skip one or more exons.

Exonization

Sequence from the inserted element is spliced into the mRNA as a new exon or an exon extension.

Aberrant splicing

Other splice changes: cryptic splice sites, altered splice-site choice, pseudo-exons from flanking sequence.

What the papers report

1 report
Hinderhofer et al. 2014 · PLoS One · PMID 24621962 · coded from full text
Identification of a new intronic BMPR2-mutation and early diagnosis of heritable pulmonary arterial hypertension in a large family with mean clinical follow-up of 12 years.
Exon skippingExonizationAberrant splicing Heterozygous antisense AluYb8 26 bp upstream of exon 6 (increasing branch point-acceptor distance) causes skipping of exon 6, use of a cryptic acceptor deleting the first 93 nt of exon 6, and inclusion of intron 5 plus Alu sequence upstream of exon 6. Found in 16 relatives (6 of 15 carriers with HPAH; penetrance ~40%).
“Cloning of the different PCR products and sequencing analysis revealed the presence of the wild type transcript with the entire exon 6, and of four aberrant transcripts.”
