What the papers report

Del Gobbo et al. 2024 · Am J Med Genet A · PMID 38131126 · coded from abstract
Long-read genome sequencing reveals a novel intronic retroelement insertion in NR5A1 associated with 46,XY differences of sexual development.
Expression loss A rare SVA in NR5A1 intron 4 found by PacBio HiFi segregated with 46,XY DSD in a multi-generational family and was associated with loss of expression of the allele in cis.
“The insertion segregated among affected family members and was associated with loss-of-expression of alleles in cis, demonstrating a functional impact on NR5A1.”
