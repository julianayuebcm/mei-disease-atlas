What the papers report

5 reports
Wimmer et al. 2011 · PLoS Genet · PMID 22125493 · coded from full text
The NF1 gene contains hotspots for L1 endonuclease-dependent de novo insertion.
ExonizationAberrant splicing A cryptic 5' splice site inside the 5'-truncated AluYb8 in exon 12 (10a) is used, so transcripts contain the first 61 nt of the Alu and lack the last 38 nt of exon 12 (r.1355_1392delins61).
“A cryptic 5′ splice site within this truncated Alu element is used instead of the natural one of the exon 12 (10a), leading to transcripts containing the first 61 nucleotides of this Alu element but lacking the 3′ half of exon 12 (10a), downstream of the Alu integration site (type 5 effect in Figure 3).”
