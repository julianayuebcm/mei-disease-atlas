de la Morena-Barrio et al. 2022 · Thromb Haemost · PMID 35764313 · coded from full text
Long-Read Sequencing Identifies the First Retrotransposon Insertion and Resolves Structural Variants Causing Antithrombin Deficiency.
Not described A 2,440-bp antisense SVA in SERPINC1 intron 6 found by nanopore sequencing in two unrelated probands co-segregates with antithrombin deficiency; the authors speculate transcriptional interference or altered splicing but the exact mechanism could not be investigated.
“Remarkably, two cases (P9 and P10) presented an insertion of 2,440 bp in intron 6.”
de la Morena-Barrio et al. 2022 · J Mol Diagn · PMID 35218943 · coded from abstract · low confidence
Molecular Dissection of Structural Variations Involved in Antithrombin Deficiency.
Not described Among structural variants in 39 antithrombin-deficient patients, retrotransposon insertions in SERPINC1 were detected, one with a founder effect; type, location and mechanism are not given in the abstract.
“found three structural variants with a founder effect: the insertion of a retrotransposon, duplication of exon 6, and a 20-gene deletion.”
