What the papers report

1 report
Arman et al. 2014 · Orphanet J Rare Dis · PMID 24767306 · coded from full text
Cathepsin K analysis in a pycnodysostosis cohort: demographic, genotypic and phenotypic features.
Exonization A homozygous 301-bp antisense Alu inserted 14 bp upstream of exon 8 creates a predicted new splice acceptor at position 38 of the insert; the authors predict (in silico only, no RNA study) inclusion of Alu sequence before exon 8 leading to a stop codon after 54 unrelated amino acids.
“Furthermore, the insertion showed new potential splicing acceptor site at position 38 of the insertion sequence with a consensus value of 89.2%, and, consensus value for formal (wild type) acceptor site was 87.8% on Human Splicing Finder.”
