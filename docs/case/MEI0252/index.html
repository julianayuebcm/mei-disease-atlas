What the papers report

1 report
van der Klift et al. 2012 · Hum Mutat · PMID 22461402 · coded from abstract
Insertion of an SVA element, a nonautonomous retrotransposon, in PMS2 intron 7 as a novel cause of Lynch syndrome.
Other aberrant transcript A 2.2 kb 5'-truncated SVA_F in PMS2 intron 7 produces an aberrant transcript that is degraded by NMD; its nature was only resolved in RNA from lymphocytes with NMD inhibited (exact splice outcome not given in abstract).
“The molecular nature of this insertion could only be resolved in RNA from cultured lymphocytes in which nonsense-mediated RNA decay was inhibited.”
