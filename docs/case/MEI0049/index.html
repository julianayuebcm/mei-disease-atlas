What the papers report

1 report
Baba et al. 2026 · Pediatr Allergy Immunol · PMID 42432877 · coded from full text
Long-read sequencing reveals SVA insertion in AP3B1 causing Hermansky-Pudlak syndrome 2.
Premature polyadenylation A maternally inherited ~2.63-kb SVA in AP3B1 intron 7, in trans with a paternal exon 8-11 deletion, activates a cryptic polyadenylation site so that transcripts terminate shortly after exon 7 (3' RACE long-read cDNA and RNA-seq), reading 25 codons into intron 7 before a stop.
“In our patient, SVA insertion activates a cryptic polyadenylation site causing premature termination of transcription, which has been described in L1 but not SVA retrotransposons.”
