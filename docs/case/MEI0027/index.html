What the papers report

Wilund et al. 2002 · Hum Mol Genet · PMID 12417523 · coded from abstract
Molecular mechanisms of autosomal recessive hypercholesterolemia.
Expression loss An SVA retroposon insertion in ARH intron 1 was the only mutation found in one proband and was associated with no detectable ARH mRNA and no full-length protein.
“The only mutation identified in the remaining proband was a SINE VNTR Alu (SVA) retroposon insertion in intron 1, which was associated with no detectable ARH mRNA.”
Taniguchi-Ikeda et al. 2011 · Nature · PMID 21979053 · coded from full text
Pathogenic exon-trapping by SVA retrotransposon and rescue in Fukuyama muscular dystrophy.
Exonization Previously reported disease-associated SVA insertion; RT-PCR of patient fibroblast cDNA showed splicing into the SVA (exon-trapping), as for FCMD.
“Moreover, we observe exon-trapping in other SVA insertions associated with disease (hypercholesterolemia4, neutral lipid storage disease5) and human-specific SVA insertion in a novel gene.”
