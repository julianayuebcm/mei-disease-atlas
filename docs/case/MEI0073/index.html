What the papers report

1 report
Tavares et al. 2019 · Mol Genet Genomic Med · PMID 30484961 · coded from full text
Retrotransposon insertion as a novel mutational event in Bardet-Biedl syndrome.
Coding disruption A paternally inherited ~1.7-kb SVA (SVA_F-like) in BBS1 exon 13, in trans with p.Met390Arg; the SVA-containing allele is expressed in patient LCLs and predicted to encode BBS1 up to residue 406 followed by 46 SVA-derived residues and a stop codon.
“these results showed that BBS1 protein is translated up to AA residue 406, followed by insertion of 46 new AA residues (corresponding to the SVA) before reaching stop codon”
