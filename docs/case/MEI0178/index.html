An Alu-mediated rearrangement as cause of exon skipping in Hunter disease.
Exon skippingInsertion-associated deletion A ~3-kb deletion of IDS sequence accompanied by a 157-bp insert partly matching an AluS; the authors propose Alu retrotransposition plus Alu-mediated deletion, and the rearrangement causes skipping of exon 8 in cDNA. Insertion origin is only partly resolved (only 83 bp recognized as Alu).
“both an Alu retrotransposition and an Alu-mediated deletion underlie the disease-producing rearrangement”
