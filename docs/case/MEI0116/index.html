What the papers report

1 report
Pozojevic et al. 2026 · Eur J Hum Genet · PMID 42732008 · coded from abstract
Long-read sequencing reveals a hidden Alu-mediated splice defect in CPLANE1, causing orofaciodigital syndrome type VI.
Exon skippingExpression loss An Alu insertion close to an exon-intron boundary of CPLANE1, in trans with c.8633-4_8633-3del, was found only by long-read sequencing; transcript analysis showed it causes exon skipping, with reduced total CPLANE1 mRNA in patient fibroblasts.
“Transcript analysis showed that each variant independently resulted in exon skipping, and quantitative expression studies revealed reduced total CPLANE1 mRNA levels in patient-derived fibroblasts.”
