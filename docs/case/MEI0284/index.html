Retrotransposon insertion as a novel mutational cause of spinal muscular atrophy.
ExonizationExpression loss An SVA_F in SMN1 intron 7 produces a mutant FL-SMN1-SVA transcript with exon extension into SVA sequence that is degraded by NMD, sharply reducing full-length SMN1 transcript.
“We described the mutant FL-SMN1-SVA transcript characterized by exon extension and showed that it is subject to degradation by nonsense-mediated mRNA decay.”
